=== tfsa or rrsp for a student?
Both are registered accounts that help your savings grow with less tax, but they work in opposite ways.

**TFSA (Tax-Free Savings Account)**
- You contribute money you've already paid tax on, so there's no deduction.
- Growth and withdrawals are **tax-free**.
- You can withdraw any time, and the amount you take out is added back to your room on January 1 of the next year.

**RRSP (Registered Retirement Savings Plan)**
- Contributions are **tax-deductible**, which lowers your income for the year.
- Growth is tax-sheltered, but withdrawals are taxed as income.
- It's meant for retirement. It must be converted (usually by age 71) or you may be charged a penalty.

**Which one fits you?**
- **Lower income and shorter-term goals:** a TFSA.
- **Higher income and longer-term goals:** an RRSP contribution.
- **Saving for something in between:** a TFSA with extra savings elsewhere.

Contribution limits change every year, and your personal room is shown in CRA My Account. If your situation is complex, a fee-only financial planner can help.
